# Manu Smriti — मनुस्मृतिः

**Status: ✅ Digitized and translated (100% of verses extracted)**

## About the Text
The *Manu Smriti* (also known as the *Manava Dharmashastra*) is one of the most important and authoritative texts of the Hindu Dharmashastra tradition. It outlines the laws, social duties (dharma), rites of passage, and ethical guidelines for human society.

## Digitization Notes
- The source text was a continuous 2.8 MB text file containing both the original verses and Kulluka Bhatta's commentary (Manvarthamuktavali).
- An AI-assisted pipeline was used to isolate the 2,684 original verses from the commentary, and automatically translate them into English and Hindi.
- **Note on Chapter Alignment:** Because the raw text was processed in overlapping chunks, the automatic assignment of verses to their respective 12 chapters is approximate. The files (`MS_001.json` through `MS_012.json`) contain all the verses, but a manual pass is required to perfectly align the verse numbers and chapter boundaries.
