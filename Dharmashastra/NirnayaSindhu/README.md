# Nirnaya Sindhu — निर्णयसिन्धु

**Author:** Kamalakara Bhatta
**Period:** 1612 CE
**Status: Not yet digitized in this collection**

The authoritative Nibandha on **kala-nirnaya** — the determination of the correct
time/day for festivals, vratas, and samskaras. It is the source most panchang
compilers rely on for tithi-pervasion (vyapini) rules: which tithi governs a
festival when the tithi spans parts of two civil days, how a kshaya (clipped) or
vriddhi (doubled) tithi is resolved, and the festival-specific exceptions.

**Why this is the priority festival addition.** The AstroAcharya festivals
drik-exact catalog implements exactly these rules — Vijayadashami (Dashami
pervading aparahna), Holika Dahan (Bhadra avoidance), Diwali (Amavasya pradosha
pervasion), sankranti civil-date assignment. They are currently pinned to
drikpanchang convention; this text is the classical authority behind that
convention, so digitizing it lets the festival rules cite shlokas.

Also backs the **"Dharmasindhu" durmuhurta school** indirectly — Dharmasindhu
(separate folder) derives much of its muhurta material from this Nirnaya-Sindhu
tradition.

Available at: Internet Archive (Nirnaya Sagar Press edition); Sanskrit Documents.
