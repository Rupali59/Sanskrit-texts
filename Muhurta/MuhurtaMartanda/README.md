# Muhurta Martanda — मुहूर्तमार्तण्ड

**Author:** Narayana Daivajna (son of Ananta)
**Period:** 1571 CE
**Status: Not yet digitized in this collection**

A second major muhurta manual, slightly predating *Muhurta Chintamani* and often
read alongside it. Valuable here as an **independent cross-check** on the muhurta
windows: where two manuals agree, a window can be shown with confidence; where
they diverge (as the Durmuhurta weekday positions do across schools), the
divergence itself is the thing to surface for Vipin's review rather than picking
one silently.

Secondary priority — add after `MuhurtaChintamani`, which is the primary cited
source. Useful specifically for adjudicating the multi-school Durmuhurta tables in
`astroacharya/.../calculations/muhurta/durmuhurta.py`.

Available at: Internet Archive; Sanskrit Documents Jyotisha section; Chowkhamba
editions (with the *Manorama* commentary).
