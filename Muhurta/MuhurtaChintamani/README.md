# Muhurta Chintamani — मुहूर्तचिन्तामणि

**Author:** Rama Daivajna (son of Ananta Daivajna)
**Period:** 1600 CE
**Status: Not yet digitized in this collection**

The standard reference manual of muhurta (electional astrology) across North
Indian panchang practice. Covers the day-segment scheme (the 30 muhurtas of day
and night), Choghadiya, Durmuhurta, the kaala-velas, Abhijit, dishaashool, and
the tithi/nakshatra/yoga/karana suitability tables for specific undertakings.
Usually studied with the *Piyusha-dhara* commentary of Govinda Daivajna.

**Why this text is the priority addition.** It is the cited source for almost the
entire AstroAcharya muhurta surface that currently has no corpus backing:

- `calculations/muhurta/choghadiya.py` — Choghadiya 8-cell day/night scheme
- `calculations/muhurta/day_segments.py` + `data/thirty_muhurtas.json` — the
  30-muhurta enumeration (Rudra, Ahi, Mitra …) and Abhijit/Brahma windows
- `calculations/muhurta/durmuhurta.py` — the "Muhurta Chintamani" durmuhurta
  weekday table (the primary of the three schools shown)
- `calculations/muhurta/dishaashool.py` — forbidden travel direction by weekday

Digitizing this text lets those `@source(("Muhurta_Chintamani", …))` citations be
verified against shlokas rather than asserted against an absent text.

Available at: Internet Archive, Chowkhamba / Motilal Banarsidass editions;
Sanskrit Documents Jyotisha section.
